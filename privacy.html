Privacy

Your privacy

The short version: this website doesn't collect anything from you.

Visiting the site

There are no cookies, no contact forms, no sign-ups and no visitor tracking or statistics on this website. We don't know who visits and we don't ask.

The fonts and photographs are all stored on this site itself, so your browser doesn't contact Google or any other outside service to show them.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may briefly log the technical details of each visit (such as your IP address) to keep its service secure. The club never sees these. You can read GitHub's privacy statement for more.

Links to Facebook or other sites take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Members' names and photographs

We show the names of our office bearers, and the names of photographers alongside their photographs, so that visitors know who runs the club and so that every photographer is credited for their work. Hidden information stored inside photo files, such as GPS location, is removed before they go online.

If you would like your name or a photograph taken down, or anything corrected, just ask and we'll do it promptly.
