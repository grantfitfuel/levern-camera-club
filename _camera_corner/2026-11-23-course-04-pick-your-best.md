Most shoots have a handful of strong photographs and many that are nearly there. The skill is spotting the handful quickly, then putting your editing time into them.

## Getting ready

1. In Lightroom Classic, press **G** to go to the grid in the **Library** module
2. In the left panels, click the folder (under **Folders**) or the import (under **Catalog, Previous Import**) you want to sort
3. Click the first photograph, then press **E** to see it large
4. Press **Shift+Tab** to hide the panels so the photograph fills the screen. Press it again later to get them back

## The views

- **G** grid view, to see the whole shoot at once
- **E** loupe view, one photograph filling the screen
- **C** compare view, two photographs side by side
- **N** survey view, several selected photographs together
- **Z** zooms to 100% to check focus, and back again
- The **left and right arrow keys** move to the previous and next photograph

## Flags, stars and colours

- **P** flags a pick (a white flag), **X** marks a reject (a black flag, and the photograph greys out), **U** removes the flag
- **1 to 5** give stars, **0** removes them
- **6 to 9** add colour labels, which some people use for "to print" or "for competition"

The flag and stars appear under the photograph in the grid, and on the toolbar under the photograph in loupe view. You can click them there too.

Turn on **Caps Lock** and Lightroom moves to the next photograph each time you flag or rate. Culling becomes a rhythm: look, press a key, move on. Turn Caps Lock off when you are done, or it will keep jumping.

## A two-pass cull, step by step

### First pass: picks and rejects

1. In loupe view (**E**), with Caps Lock on, start at the first photograph
2. Press **P** for anything with promise and **X** for anything clearly wrong: missed focus, closed eyes, a near-duplicate. Press the **right arrow** to skip anything you are unsure about
3. Do not linger. A second or two each is enough

### Second pass: stars on the picks

1. Press **G** for the grid, then **\\** (backslash) to show the **Library Filter** bar at the top
2. Click **Attribute**, then click the **white flag** beside "Flag". Only your picks show now
3. Click the first one and press **E**. Give the good ones **3** stars and the very best **5**

### Choosing between two similar frames

1. In the grid, click one photograph and **Ctrl-click** (Cmd-click on a Mac) the other
2. Press **C**. They appear side by side
3. Press **Z** to zoom both and check focus and expressions. Flag the weaker one with **X**

### Clearing the rejects

1. Click **None** in the filter bar so every photograph shows again
2. Press **Ctrl+Backspace** (Cmd+Delete on a Mac). Lightroom selects all the rejects and asks what to do
3. **Remove** takes them out of the catalog but leaves the files on the drive. **Delete from Disk** deletes the files too. If unsure, choose Remove

To see only your best, set the filter bar to **Attribute** and click the fifth star. Those are the ones to edit.

## Being honest

- Check focus at 100%. A photograph that looks sharp small may not be
- Leave it a day. Your favourite on the day is often the one with the best memory attached, not the best picture
- Judge it as a stranger would. Would it make sense to someone who was not there?
