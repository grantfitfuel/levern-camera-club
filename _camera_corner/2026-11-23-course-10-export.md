A beautiful edit can still look wrong if it is exported at the wrong size or in the wrong colour space. Exporting creates a new file from your edit; the original and the edit stay untouched in Lightroom.

## Exporting from Lightroom Classic, step by step

1. In the **Library** grid, select the photographs to export. Click one, then Ctrl-click (Cmd-click) any others
2. Click **Export** at the bottom of the left panels, or choose **File, Export**, or press **Ctrl+Shift+E** (Cmd+Shift+E on a Mac)
3. At the top of the window, check **Export To** says **Hard Drive**
4. Work down the sections in the middle of the window, described below. Click a section's name to open it

### Export Location

Set **Export To** to **Specific folder**, click **Choose** and pick a folder you will find again, such as an Exports folder in Pictures. Tick **Put in Subfolder** and name it after the purpose, such as "Club competition".

### File Naming

Tick **Rename To** and choose a pattern. For a set format such as title and photographer name, choose **Edit** from the menu, click in the box and type your name after the token Lightroom offers, or choose **Custom Name** and type it.

### File Settings

- **Image Format:** JPEG
- **Quality:** 80 to 90. It looks the same as 100 and the file is much smaller
- **Color Space:** **sRGB**. This is what screens, projectors and the web expect

### Image Sizing

Tick **Resize to Fit**. Choose **Long Edge** and type the size the competition asks for, such as **2000 pixels**. If a projected competition asks for a set width and height, such as 1600 by 1200, choose **Width & Height** and type both. Leave **Don't Enlarge** ticked.

### Output Sharpening

Tick **Sharpen For**, choose **Screen** and **Standard**. For print, choose **Matte Paper** or **Glossy Paper** to match the paper.

### Metadata

**Copyright & Contact Info Only** keeps your name in the file without sharing camera details. Tick **Remove Location Info** if you prefer.

### Watermarking

Optional. Leave it unticked for competitions, which usually do not allow them.

### Finally

Click **Export** at the bottom right. A progress bar runs at the top left of Lightroom

## Saving it as a preset

Once the settings are right, before clicking Export:

1. Click **Add** at the bottom left of the Export window
2. Name it, such as "Club competition" or "Social media", and click **Create**

Next time, right-click a photograph, choose **Export**, and pick the preset from the list. Nothing to remember.

## Exporting from Lightroom on a phone

1. Open the photograph
2. Tap the **share icon** (a square with an arrow) at the top
3. Choose **Export as**
4. Set **File type** to JPG, **Dimensions** to a size such as 2048 px or Custom, and **Quality** to around 85
5. Tap the tick, then choose where to send or save it

## Print

For printing at home or at a lab, export as TIFF or high quality JPEG at full size (untick Resize to Fit). Ask the lab which colour space it wants; sRGB is the safe choice if unsure. A calibrated screen matters most here, so prints match what you saw. The Monitor calibration lesson explains how.

## Before you send it

- Open the exported file outside Lightroom and look at it once more
- Check the dimensions and file size against the rules. On Windows, right-click the file and choose **Properties, Details**. On a Mac, select it and press **Cmd+I**
- Check the file name

The Exporting for screen and print lesson goes further into sizes and print settings.
