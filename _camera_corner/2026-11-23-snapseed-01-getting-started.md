Snapseed is a free editing app from Google for iPhone and Android phones. There is no subscription, no watermark and nothing to buy inside the app. It is a good choice if you take most of your photographs on a phone, or want to edit camera photographs on the move.

Snapseed 4, released in May 2026 for both iPhone and Android, redesigned the app. These tips follow version 4. If your phone still has an older version, update it from the App Store or Google Play; older versions look different.

## Opening a photograph

1. Install **Snapseed** from the App Store or Google Play and open it
2. The home screen shows photographs you have edited before. Tap the option to open a new photograph and choose one from your library (the exact button varies; the floating camera button opens Snapseed's own camera instead)
3. The photograph opens with the editing tabs along the bottom

## The three tabs

Along the bottom of the screen:

- **Looks:** ready-made styles. Tap one to preview it. Useful as a starting point, not a finish
- **Tools:** every tool, sorted into groups: **Refine**, **Fix**, **Style** and **All**. If you cannot find a tool, look in **All**. Tools you use often can be kept on a favourites bar
- **Export:** saving and sharing (see below)

## Using a tool

Every adjustment tool works the same way:

1. Tap **Tools**, then a tool, such as **Tune Image** (brightness, contrast and so on)
2. **Swipe up or down** on the photograph to choose which setting to change, such as Brightness, Contrast or Saturation. A list appears while your finger is down
3. **Drag left or right** to change the amount. The number appears on screen
4. Tap the **tick** to keep the change, or the **cross** to cancel it

To compare, **press and hold** on the photograph to see it without the current tool's change.

## Undo and changing your mind

Snapseed keeps every tool you use as a separate step, so nothing is final.

- Tap the **edit stack** icon at the top of the screen (it looks like a stack of layers). It offers **Undo**, **Redo** and **Revert** (back to the original)
- The same menu opens the list of steps (called the stack). Tap a step to change its settings again, or delete it

## Saving

Tap **Export**:

- **iPhone:** **Save** (replaces the photograph in your library with the edit, which Snapseed can still undo later), **Save a copy** (keeps the original and adds the edit as a new photograph, the safest choice), **Export** (a separate copy) and **Share**
- **Android:** **Save** and **Share**. Save keeps the original and saves the edit as a new photograph

Snapseed's **Settings** let you choose the size and quality of saved files. For club entries, choose the largest size and high quality, then check the competition's rules.

> Small screen, big results. Keep it subtle.
