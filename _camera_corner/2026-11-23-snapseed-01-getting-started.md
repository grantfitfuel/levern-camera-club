Snapseed is a free editing app from Google for iPhone, iPad and Android phones. There are no adverts and no subscription. It is a good choice if you take most of your photographs on a phone, or want to edit camera photographs on the move.

The iPhone version was redesigned in 2025 (version 3). The Android version may look a little different depending on when it was last updated, but the tools have the same names.

## Opening a photograph

1. Install **Snapseed** from the App Store or Google Play and open it
2. Tap the **+** button (or tap anywhere on the empty screen the first time) and choose a photograph from your library
3. The photograph opens with the editing tabs along the bottom

## The three tabs

On the newer version the bottom of the screen has three tabs:

- **Looks:** ready-made styles. Tap one to preview it. Useful as a starting point, not a finish
- **Faves:** the tools you use most, for quick access. You choose what goes here
- **Tools:** every tool. This is where most of the work happens

On older versions, **Looks**, **Tools** and **Export** sit along the bottom instead.

## Using a tool

Every adjustment tool works the same way:

1. Tap **Tools**, then a tool, such as **Tune Image**
2. **Swipe up or down** on the photograph to choose which setting to change, such as Brightness, Contrast or Saturation. A list appears while your finger is down
3. **Swipe left or right** to change the amount. The number appears at the top. On the newer version you can also drag the curved slider at the bottom
4. Tap the **tick** to keep the change, or the **cross** to cancel it

To compare, **press and hold** on the photograph to see it without the current tool's change.

## Undo and changing your mind

Snapseed keeps every tool you use as a separate step, so nothing is final.

- Look for the **undo** arrow, or the **edit stack** icon (layers) at the top of the screen. It offers **Undo**, **Redo**, **Revert** (back to the original) and **View edits**
- **View edits** lists each tool you used. Tap one to change its settings again, hide it, or delete it

## Saving

Tap **Export** (top right on the newer version, bottom on older ones). The choices include:

- **Save a copy:** keeps the original and saves the edit as a new photograph. The safest choice
- **Save:** replaces the photograph with the edited version (Snapseed can still undo it later)
- **Export:** saves a JPEG copy, with the size and quality set in **Settings**
- **Share:** send it straight to another app

In **Settings** (the three dots or the menu), set **Image sizing** and **Format and quality**. For club entries, choose a large size and JPEG quality around 95%, then check the competition's rules.

> Small screen, big results. Keep it subtle.
