The same order used in Lightroom works in Snapseed: fix the foundations, set the tones, then the colour, then the details.

## 1. Crop and straighten

- **Crop:** choose a ratio at the bottom (Free, Original, 1:1, 4:3 and so on), then drag the corners
- **Rotate:** Snapseed often straightens the horizon automatically. Swipe to adjust; the arrows rotate by 90 degrees
- **Perspective:** fixes leaning buildings. Choose Tilt and swipe

## 2. Tone and colour with Tune Image

In **Tune Image**, swipe up or down to choose and left or right to adjust:

- **Brightness:** overall brightness
- **Contrast**
- **Highlights:** swipe left to bring back a bright sky
- **Shadows:** swipe right to open up dark areas
- **Ambiance:** a mix of contrast and saturation. Powerful; keep it low
- **Saturation**
- **Warmth:** cooler (left) or warmer (right)

For a neutral colour cast, the **White Balance** tool has an eyedropper: tap the icon and place it on something grey.

**Curves** gives finer control over contrast: drag a point on the line up to brighten or down to darken.

## 3. Detail

- **Details:** **Structure** adds texture; **Sharpening** sharpens. Zoom in (pinch) to judge them. Keep both modest

## 4. Local edits

This is where a phone edit starts to look considered.

- **Brush:** paint an effect on just one area. Choose **Dodge & Burn**, **Exposure**, **Temperature** or **Saturation** at the bottom, set the amount with the arrows, then paint with your finger. Pinch to zoom in for accuracy. Set the amount to **Eraser** to remove it. Tap the **eye** icon to see where you have painted
- **Selective:** tap **+** and tap a spot on the photograph. A control point appears; pinch to change its size (the red area shows what it affects), then swipe up or down to choose Brightness, Contrast, Saturation or Structure and left or right to adjust. Good for lifting a face or darkening a patch of sky
- **Healing:** paint over a small distraction, such as a speck, wire or blemish, and it blends away. Zoom in first. Works best on small things against simple backgrounds

## 5. A finishing touch

- **Vignette:** darkens the edges gently. Tap the photograph to set the centre on your subject
- **Black & White** or **Noir** for monochrome. The colour filter buttons (red, orange, yellow, green) change how colours turn into greys, as a colour filter on a camera would

## Using a Look on several photographs

When an edit works, tap **Looks**, then **+** or **Save look** to save your steps as a Look. Open the next photograph and tap the saved Look to apply the same edit.

> Edit for the subject, not for the app.
