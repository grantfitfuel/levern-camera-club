A beautiful edit can still look wrong if it is exported at the wrong size or in the wrong colour space. Exporting creates a new file from your edit; the original and the edit stay untouched in Lightroom.

## Opening Export

Select one or more photographs and press **Ctrl+Shift+E** (Cmd+Shift+E on a Mac), or click Export at the bottom left of the Library module. In Lightroom on a phone, tap the share icon and choose Export as.

## The settings that matter

- **Export Location:** a folder you will find again, such as an Exports folder with a subfolder per purpose
- **File Naming:** many competitions want a set format, such as title and photographer name. Set it here so you never rename by hand
- **File Settings:** JPEG, **sRGB** colour space. sRGB is what screens, projectors and the web expect. Quality 80 to 90 is indistinguishable from 100 and much smaller
- **Image Sizing:** tick Resize to Fit and enter the size the competition asks for, such as Long Edge 2000 pixels. For projected competitions, check whether it asks for a set width and height, such as 1600 by 1200
- **Output Sharpening:** Screen, Standard. For print, choose Matte or Glossy to match the paper
- **Metadata:** Copyright and Contact Info Only keeps your name in the file without sharing camera details or location. Tick Remove Location Info if you prefer
- **Watermarking:** optional

## Export presets

Once the settings are right, click **Add** at the bottom left of the Export window and save them as a preset, such as "Club competition" or "Social media". Next time, right-click a photograph and choose Export, then the preset. Nothing to remember.

## Print

For printing at home or at a lab, export as TIFF or high quality JPEG at full size. Ask the lab which colour space it wants; sRGB is the safe choice if unsure. A calibrated screen matters most here, so prints match what you saw. The Monitor calibration lesson explains how.

## Before you send it

- Open the exported file outside Lightroom and look at it once more
- Check the dimensions and file size against the rules
- Check the file name
