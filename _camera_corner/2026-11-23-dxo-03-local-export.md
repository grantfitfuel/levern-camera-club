## Local adjustments

1. Click **Local Adjustments** in the top toolbar
2. **Right-click** the photograph to open the tool wheel (or use the tool bar that appears), and choose a tool:
   - **Control Point:** click on the subject. A circle appears; drag its edge to set the size. PhotoLab finds similar colours and tones within the circle and applies the change only to those. Use the small sliders beside the point, or the panel on the right, to change brightness, contrast, saturation and more. This is DxO's famous U Point technology
   - **Control Line:** like a control point, drawn as a line. Good for skies
   - **Graduated Filter:** drag down from the top for a sky
   - **Brush** and **Auto Brush:** paint where you want the effect. Auto Brush follows edges
   - **AI mask:** on recent versions, PhotoLab can select the subject, sky, people or other areas for you
3. Change the sliders in the **Local Adjustments** panel. Each mask is listed there; click its eye to hide it
4. Click **Local Adjustments** again when finished

### Dodge and burn

A control point on the subject with brightness raised slightly, and a brush at low opacity with brightness lowered on bright edges, guide the eye. Small amounts.

## Repair

Click the **Repair** tool (the plaster) in the top toolbar. Paint over dust or a small distraction. Choose **Clone** or **Repair** mode; drag the source circle if PhotoLab picks a poor area. For big removals, send the photograph to Photoshop, Affinity or GIMP.

## Export

1. Select the photographs in the filmstrip
2. Click **Export to Disk** at the bottom right (or **File, Export to Disk**)
3. Choose an option or create a new one:
   - **Format:** JPEG, quality around 90
   - **ICC Profile:** sRGB
   - **Resize:** tick it and set the long edge, such as 2000 pixels
   - **Destination folder** and **suffix**
4. Click **Export**

### DNG for another program

To use DxO's noise reduction and lens corrections and then finish in Lightroom, export as **DNG (denoising and optical corrections only)**. You get a clean RAW-like file that Lightroom treats like your original. DxO sells the same feature on its own as PureRAW.

Check each competition's rules for size and naming. The Exporting for screen and print lesson goes into more detail.

> Clean files in, careful edits, clean files out.
