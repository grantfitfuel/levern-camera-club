Our weekly competition is open to everyone, and a phone is every bit as welcome as a camera. Here are five habits that help any entry stand out.

## 1. Read the theme twice

The obvious idea is usually the one everyone else will have too. Spend a minute thinking about a second or third interpretation before you go out shooting.

## 2. Keep the background simple

A busy background pulls the eye away from your subject. Move a step to the side, get lower, or open up your aperture to soften what's behind.

## 3. Watch the light

Early morning and late afternoon light is softer and warmer. On a dull day, try shooting near a window, where the light falls gently on one side of your subject.

## 4. Fill the frame

If something doesn't add to the picture, leave it out. Get closer, or crop afterwards, so your subject is clear the moment someone looks.

## 5. Check the edges before you press the shutter

Run your eye around the edges of the frame. Stray branches, bright spots and wonky horizons are much easier to fix before you take the picture than after.
