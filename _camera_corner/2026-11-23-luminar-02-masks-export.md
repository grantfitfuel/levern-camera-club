## Masking a tool

Almost every tool in Luminar Neo can be limited to part of the photograph.

1. Open a tool, such as **Develop**, and make the change
2. At the top of the tool's panel, click **Masking**
3. Choose how to make the mask:
   - **Mask AI:** Luminar recognises parts of the photograph, such as Sky, People, Buildings, Water or Vegetation. Click the one you want
   - **Brush:** paint the area. Set **Size**, **Softness** and **Opacity**; switch to **Erase** to remove
   - **Radial:** an oval, soft at the edge
   - **Gradient:** a gradual change across the frame, like a graduated filter
   - **Object Selection:** brush roughly over something and Luminar finds its edges
4. The masked area shows in red while you work. Click **Done** when finished

To use the same tool twice, with different masks, use **Layers**: click **+** in the Layers panel to add an adjustment layer, then mask the tools on it.

### Dodge and burn

The **Dodge & Burn** tool (in **Professional**) lets you paint lightening and darkening directly. Choose **Lighten** or **Darken**, set a low **Strength**, and brush over the areas. Build it up gently.

## Removing distractions

- **Erase:** paint over a person, wire, litter or a sensor spot, then click **Erase**. Luminar fills the area. For dust there is a separate **Dust spots** option, and **Power lines** removes wires automatically
- **GenErase** (on recent versions, may need an internet connection): uses generative AI for larger removals
- **Clone:** copies from one area to another for precise work

Check the competition rules before entering a photograph with things removed or added.

## Exporting

1. Click the **Share** (or **Export**) icon at the top right and choose **Export to Disk**
2. **Format:** JPEG, quality around 90
3. **Color space:** sRGB
4. **Size:** choose **Long edge** and type the size, such as 2000 pixels
5. Choose a folder and file name, then click **Export**

Luminar keeps your edits in its catalog, so you can export again at any size later.

> Remove what distracts. Keep what is true.
