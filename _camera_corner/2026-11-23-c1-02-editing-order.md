Capture One's tools can be used in any order, but the order below avoids undoing your own work. Hover over any slider and its name appears; double-click a slider to reset it.

## 1. Foundations

- **Base Characteristics** (in the Color tool tab): **ICC Profile** should be your camera model. **Curve** is Auto or Film Standard. Leave both unless you have a reason
- **Lens Correction** (Lens tool tab): choose your lens in **Profile** if Capture One has not already. Tick or slide **Chromatic Aberration** and **Light Falloff** as needed
- **Crop:** choose the **crop** cursor tool, drag over the photograph, then click the pointer tool to finish. Choose a ratio from the **Crop** tool in the Composition tool tab
- **Straighten:** choose the **straighten** (rotate) cursor tool and draw a line along something that should be level
- **Keystone:** use the **keystone** cursor tool or the Keystone tool to correct leaning buildings

## 2. White balance

In the **White Balance** tool, click the **picker** and click on something neutral grey. Or use **Kelvin** and **Tint**. The White balance lesson explains why it matters.

## 3. Exposure and tone

Work down these tools in the Exposure tool tab:

- **Exposure** tool: **Exposure** for overall brightness, **Contrast**, **Brightness** (lifts the mid-tones) and **Saturation**
- **High Dynamic Range** tool: **Highlight** pulls back bright skies, **Shadow** opens dark areas, **White** and **Black** set the end points
- **Levels** and **Curve** for finer control. A gentle S-curve adds contrast

Watch the **histogram** at the top of the tab. To see clipped areas, turn on the **exposure warning** from the toolbar (the triangle icon) or the View menu.

## 4. Colour

- **Color Editor:** click **Basic**, then pick a colour from the list or with the picker and change its hue, saturation and lightness. **Skin Tone** has its own tab for smoothing skin colour
- **Color Balance:** tint shadows, mid-tones and highlights. Small moves

## 5. Presence and detail

- **Clarity** tool: **Clarity** for mid-tone punch, **Structure** for fine detail. Keep both modest
- **Dehaze** for haze
- **Sharpening** (Details tool tab): zoom to 100% first. The defaults are a good start
- **Noise Reduction:** raise **Luminance** for high ISO files

## Variants and copying edits

- A **variant** is an extra version of the photograph, to try something different without losing the first. In the **Image** menu, **New Variant** starts a fresh one and **Clone Variant** copies the current edits
- To copy edits to other photographs: click the **up arrow** (copy) in the toolbar on the edited photograph, select the others, and click the **down arrow** (apply).

> Fix it, then finish it.
