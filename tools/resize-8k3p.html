Resize photos for the website

Pick one or more photos. Each one is shrunk to 2000 pixels on its longest side, which is the size the website uses, so it uploads to Pages CMS without an error.

Everything happens on your own phone or computer. Your photos are not sent anywhere.

On iPhone, tap Save and then Save Image: the smaller copy goes into your Photos, ready to upload. On a computer it goes to your Downloads folder.
