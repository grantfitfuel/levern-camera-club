About the club

Photographers helping photographers

Levern Camera Club is a non-profit club based in Barrhead. We are an open, friendly group of photographers who are happy to share their knowledge with others.

Come along

If you have an interest in photography and would like to get involved, feel free to come along to Fereneze Golf Club any Wednesday evening at 7.30pm and see if our club is for you.

We meet every Wednesday from September until the end of May. Evenings mix hands-on studio sessions, talks, editing demonstrations and friendly competitions. Whether you shoot on a camera or a phone, and whether you're just starting out or have been at it for years, you'll be made welcome.
